# Playful sky interactions

Click or activate the PhotoGit title to release a wide shower of leaves falling downward in a faster 4.2-second shower. Leaves stay visible through the upper half and fade toward the bottom. The shower has no swoosh. The leaves have a small side-to-side flutter, with no sideways or diagonal sweep. Repeated title clicks let the shower finish and greet the birds again.

Click a bird for one soft two-note chirp and a small 2.6-second scatter of only its own flock. Other flocks continue flying undisturbed. The title still greets all birds. Sound only follows a gesture; the footer button mutes chirps. Click or keyboard-activate a cloud to give it a tiny jiggle within three pixels, without rotation or interrupting its drift.

Escape, hiding the tab, or leaving the page stops the effects and sound. Reduced motion suppresses the shower, bird movement, and cloud jiggle. Desktop uses 420 petals, mobile 200, and data saver caps either at 140. Evenly distributed horizontal lanes and a wider launch spread leave more space between leaves.

Run `npm run test:landing` and verify desktop/mobile clicks, keyboard cloud activation, mute, and reduced motion in the browser.
