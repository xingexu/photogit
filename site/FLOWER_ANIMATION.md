# Playful sky interactions

Click or activate the PhotoGit title to release a wide shower of leaves falling gently downward. The leaves have a small side-to-side flutter, with no sideways or diagonal sweep. Repeated title clicks let the shower finish and greet the birds again.

Click a bird for one soft two-note chirp and a small 2.6-second scatter of only its own flock. Other flocks continue flying undisturbed. The title still greets all birds. Sound only follows a gesture; the footer button mutes it. Click or keyboard-activate a cloud to give it a tiny jiggle within three pixels, without rotation or interrupting its drift.

Escape, hiding the tab, or leaving the page stops the effects and sound. Reduced motion suppresses the shower, bird movement, and cloud jiggle. Desktop uses 720 petals, mobile 360, and data saver caps either at 200.

Run `npm run test:landing` and verify desktop/mobile clicks, keyboard cloud activation, mute, and reduced motion in the browser.
